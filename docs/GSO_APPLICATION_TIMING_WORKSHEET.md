**Why we are doing this.** We want to know how long it really takes to put labels on jars and bags. Nobody is being graded. Work the way you work on a normal day.

## How to do a trial

1. **One trial = one product, one size, one kind of label.** Do not mix sizes or label types in a trial.
2. **Do 25 pieces per trial** (10 pieces for 14x16 bags). That is enough to even out a slow first piece and a late stopwatch press, and short enough to finish without a break. If the order is smaller, do what there is and write the real number.
3. **Use the stopwatch on your phone.**
4. **Setup time** = getting ready: fetching the labels and blanks, laying them out, clearing the table. Stop the watch and write it down.
5. **Application time** = start when you pick up the first label, stop when the last piece is done. Do not pause the watch for small things that happen on every job (a label that needs repositioning, a dropped piece).
6. **Normal pace. Not a race.** A fast number makes our prices wrong and next week's schedule impossible.
7. **Count defects** = pieces you had to redo or throw away. Their time stays in the trial.
8. **At least 3 trials for every row in the checklist below, done by at least 2 different people.**
9. If something was unusual (curved surface, static, label curling, cold room, new person), tick "unusual" and say what in the notes.

## How we will use this (office use)

> **Seconds per application = application time ÷ number completed.**
> Setup time is kept separate. Defects are kept separate.
>
> When the sheets are in, James chooses one of four ways to set the times:
>
> 1. **One universal time** — the same time for every label on every product.
> 2. **Per label type** — one time for body labels, one for lid labels, one for bands, one for bag labels.
> 3. **Per product size** — each jar size and each bag type has its own times.
> 4. **Baseline + override** — one normal time, with a separate time only for the sizes that are clearly slower or faster.
>
> Nothing in the system changes until James picks one. Until then every current time is **PENDING REAL SHOP TIMING VALIDATION**.
